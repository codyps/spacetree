Coverage and interpretation

Comparisons check sizes and modification times using tmutil compare -s -t -E -X. They do not read file contents to verify equality, detect metadata-only changes such as ACLs or xattrs, or measure unique storage. Added or removed directories may appear as a single subtree record; their sizes are counted once. Record counts are not necessarily file counts. Renames may appear as a removal plus an addition. Intervals may span more than one backup when snapshots are missing.

The manifest’s physical-size field is shown as recorded; it is not a promise about unique or reclaimable storage. Historical snapshots retain deleted files. No backup files are modified by this tool.
